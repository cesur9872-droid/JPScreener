      JetPhotos Guidelines Reference

      Common Rejection Reasons:

      • Centering: Aircraft must be centrally positioned vertically and horizontally unless taking up the full frame.
      • Softness / Blur: Entire fuselage and registration code must be crisp and in sharp focus.
      • Over-exposure / Under-exposure: Blown-out whites or crushed shadows with missing detail are rejected.
      • Dust Spots: Sensor dust visible in sky areas must be fully cleaned.
      • Level Horizon: Runway lines, buildings, or background sea/horizon must be level.
      • Heat Haze: Heat distortion from engines or ground reflection degrading image quality.
